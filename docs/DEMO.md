# Demonstration plan (target workflow)

This is a proposed narrative, not a report of a working demo. Use synthetic documents and verify every step on the target ATOM before presenting it as complete.

## Primary: changed requirements to reviewed plan

1. Show the ATOM-hosted Nemotron endpoint and confirm the application is using it.
2. Sign in as an authorized business analyst and upload a synthetic client brief.
3. Extract requirements, ambiguities, user stories, acceptance criteria, and citations to source passages.
4. Ask the triage workflow for effort ranges, dependencies, and staffing recommendations. Explain uncertainty and assumptions.
5. Upload a revised brief with a changed requirement; show which proposals changed and compare priorities before and after.
6. Have a human review and approve proposed changes; show the resulting audit record.
7. If feasible, demonstrate the same core workflow while disconnected from public internet.

## Secondary scenes

- A new employee asks a policy question and receives a cited answer from authorized local documents.
- An employee submits a synthetic receipt; the system drafts a claim and routes it for approval without authorizing payment.
- An executive requests a budget variance explanation grounded in synthetic financial records, with calculations produced by deterministic code.

## Evidence to capture

Record model/runtime identity, local endpoint use, source citations, authorization checks, approval steps, audit records, and offline behavior. Do not fabricate model telemetry, financial data, citations, or successful steps. If a step fails, disclose it and narrow the demo claim.
