# IT Security Policy
audience: all

## SEC-1 Client documents
Client documents must stay on the ATOM server and approved company systems. Do not upload client documents to public AI tools or personal cloud storage.

## SEC-2 Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all company accounts, including email, source control and the HR system.

## SEC-3 Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents, lost devices or data leaks must be reported to the DevSecOps team within 24 hours of discovery.

## SEC-4 PDPA and personal data
Under PDPA, never share IC numbers or other personal identifiers in chat or email. Use the approved secure systems for any personal data processing.

## SEC-5 Need-to-know access
Access to project data is granted on a need-to-know basis. Employees may only access projects they are assigned to.
