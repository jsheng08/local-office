# Expense Claims Policy
audience: all

## EX-1 Submission deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, together with an itemised receipt. Claims without an itemised receipt will be rejected.

## EX-2 Per-claim limits
Per-claim limits in MYR: meals RM 60 per day; client entertainment RM 200 per claim (requires PM pre-approval); local travel including Grab RM 150 per claim; mileage RM 0.60 per km; accommodation RM 300 per night; software purchases RM 500 per claim.

## EX-3 Claims above limit
Any claim above the applicable limit in EX-2 requires approval from both Finance and the employee's project manager before reimbursement.

## EX-4 Non-claimable items
Alcohol and personal items are not claimable under any category. Receipts containing such items must have them excluded from the claimed amount.

## EX-5 SST
Receipts must show the SST amount and the merchant's SST registration number where SST is charged.
