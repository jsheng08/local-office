# Leave Policy
audience: all

## LV-1 Annual leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 14 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. After 2 years of continuous service, the entitlement increases to 16 days per year.

## LV-2 Medical leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid medical leave per year. A medical certificate (MC) from a registered medical practitioner is required for every day of medical leave.

## LV-3 Applying for leave
Annual leave must be applied for in the HR system at least 7 days in advance and approved by your project manager. Emergency leave is handled case by case.

## LV-4 Carry forward
Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December.

## LV-5 Public holidays
Public holidays follow the gazetted holidays of the state in which the employee is based. Employees working on a public holiday at a client's request receive a replacement day off.
