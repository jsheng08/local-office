# Sinar Pantai Logistics Sdn Bhd (SPL)

**What they do:** last-mile logistics with a strong east-coast Malaysia footprint (Kelantan, Terengganu, Pahang). Per brief v1 they run **40 depots and around 600 delivery drivers** across Peninsular Malaysia. A large share of parcels is [[COD]], with drivers collecting cash at the door.

**The pain:** dispatch runs on phone calls and spreadsheets. Parcels are missed, rural drivers lose coverage, and management cannot see where anyone is. Customers phone depots to ask "where is my parcel?".

**What they bought:** the [[PRJ-SPL Driver and Dispatch Portal]], a driver app, dispatcher dashboard and customer notifications in one portal. Contract value RM 480,000 (ceiling, per brief §10).
