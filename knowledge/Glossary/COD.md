# COD

Cash on delivery. The driver collects payment at the door. SPL verifies the recipient's identity using an [[IC Number]] (brief §8) and v2 requires e-invoices for COD receipts ([[LHDN MyInvois]]).

See [[Sinar Pantai Logistics]].
