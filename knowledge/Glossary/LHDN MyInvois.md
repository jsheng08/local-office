# LHDN MyInvois

Malaysia's national e-invoicing system run by the tax authority (LHDN). SPL's brief v2 §11 makes it mandatory by 2027-01-01 for COD receipts.

See [[RISK-002 MyInvois deadline]], [[Wong Kah Seng]].
