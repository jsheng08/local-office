# Change Request Process

1. A change arrives (e.g. [[SPL Brief v2 Changes]]).
2. The analyst ([[Aisyah Rahman]]) maps it to the baseline with clause citations. Analysts cannot approve.
3. Estimates are given as hour ranges with assumptions, not single numbers.
4. **[[Daniel Tan]] (PM) approves** before anything goes to the client or changes the board, staffing or priorities.
5. If total spend would exceed the RM 480,000 ceiling, SPL management must approve (§10).
6. Record the decision as an ADR when it is architectural.

> [!warning] No free extras
> Past absorbed changes are why [[RISK-004 Labour overrun]] exists.
