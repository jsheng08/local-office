# Leave guide

- 14 days annual leave a year, 16 after 2 years' service (LV-1).
- 14 days medical leave a year; an MC is required for every day (LV-2).
- Annual leave: apply 7 days ahead in the HR system, PM approves (LV-3). Emergency leave is case by case.
- Carry forward up to 5 days; the rest is forfeited on 31 December (LV-4).
- Public holidays follow the employee's state (LV-5).

Current leave: [[Nurul Izzah]] (annual) and [[Arjun Pillai]] (medical), see [[Blockers and Availability]]. Approver: [[Daniel Tan]].
