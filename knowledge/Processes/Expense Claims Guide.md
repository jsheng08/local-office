# Expense claims guide

Summary of the expense policy (see the policy document for exact wording).
- Submit within 30 days with an itemised receipt (EX-1).
- Limits: meals RM 60 a day; client entertainment RM 200 a claim with PM pre-approval; local travel including Grab RM 150; mileage RM 0.60 per km; accommodation RM 300 a night; software RM 500 (EX-2).
- Above-limit claims need Finance and your PM (EX-3).
- No alcohol or personal items (EX-4); receipts show SST and registration number (EX-5).
- Finance ([[Office Roles - Finance HR Exec]]) reviews; the helper drafts but never approves payments.
